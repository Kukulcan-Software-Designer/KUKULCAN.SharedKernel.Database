# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to a professional, respectful and technically constructive community around `KUKULCAN.SharedKernel.Database`.

## Our Standards

Expected behavior includes respectful technical discussion, constructive criticism, clear communication about architectural trade-offs and respect for different levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats and deliberately disruptive conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to repositories, issues, discussions, pull requests and other project-related spaces.

## Reporting

Contact project maintainers privately for conduct concerns. Security vulnerabilities must follow `SECURITY.md`.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take proportionate action, including correction, warning, temporary restriction or permanent exclusion.
